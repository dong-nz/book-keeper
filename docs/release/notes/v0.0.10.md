# Release v0.0.10 — November 17, 2026

This release gives the PDF reader a **new continuous reading mode** — pages in one horizontal row, read by scrolling sideways — brings **conveniences into the reader window** (a contents panel you can pin open, and a settings menu with the theme switch), makes **list covers bigger**, and teaches the app to **keep itself updated**.

## What changed for you

**A fourth way to read PDFs**

- The reader's view control now offers **Continuous horizontal**: pages line up in a single row at equal height and you read by scrolling sideways — a film strip of pages, useful for wide documents and anything you'd rather flow through than flip through. `→` and `←` move a page, `↑` `↓` scroll the row, zoom and the page field work as in the other modes. The three earlier modes (single page, continuous vertical, facing spreads) are unchanged.

**A contents panel that stays**

- In both readers — PDFs and books — the contents panel now has a **pin**. Pinned, the panel docks to the side of the window and stays open while you read and navigate; the text narrows to make room. Unpinning happens only through the pin itself, so the panel never disappears on its own. Hiding and showing the panel keeps the position you left it in, and the pin control's icon shows which state it's in.

**Settings in the reader window**

- Every reader window now carries the app's settings menu at the top right, next to the window buttons — the same complete menu as the main window. You can **switch the theme (Auto / Light / Dark) while reading**, and the change applies live to every open window; **Settings** opens the app's settings dialog right there.

**Bigger covers in list view**

- List view now shows a larger cover thumbnail beside each document — enough more to recognize a document by its cover without switching to grid view.

**The app keeps itself updated**

- On startup the app quietly checks whether a newer version is published. You notice nothing when there's nothing new or the check can't reach its source. When a newer version is found, a notification appears with the version and its release notes and an **Install** button: the app downloads the update with visible progress and hands it to your system's installer, closing itself for the installation.
- The settings menu also has **Check for updates**, which runs the same check when you ask and always tells you the result — including "You're up to date".

## What this version does not do yet

- The horizontal reading mode always flows left to right; right-to-left documents (Arabic or Hebrew) keep their content order but the sideways reading direction follows the page — proper right-to-left scrolling is planned for a later release.
- Updates are never installed without your click, and the app doesn't re-check while it's running — the check happens at startup or when you ask.
- The pinned contents panel is remembered while the window is open, but not across windows: a new reader window starts unpinned.
- The app still doesn't remember where you stopped reading — the same gap as before, for both PDFs and books.
- Windows only, as before — macOS and Linux builds come with a later release.

## Known issues

None known at release time. Verification for this release covered everything it changed — the automated flows for reading (both document types), the list-view layout, and the update flow tested against a trial update feed (240 checks across the reading flows plus the update flow's own checks, all passing) and a manual review of the app. The full regression pass that covers everything else runs after the release, as usual; anything it finds will be reported and scheduled.
